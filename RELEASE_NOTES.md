# Chore Quest 2.7.4

## What's new
- **Proper version numbers** in HACS, with these notes on the update screen.
- **Per-person "for tonight" count** in each racer's header ("2 for tonight" during the day, "for tomorrow" at night).
- **Pick any times of day** for a chore: tap Morning, Afternoon, Evening and Night in any combination (Morning only is now one tap).
- **Overview tile** (`mode: summary`): a compact at-a-glance tile for Home screens that opens the full Chores page. Live on the tablet and phone dashboards.
- **New CQ icon** for the integration.

## Recently
- Day and night quests follow the time-of-day sensor.
- The chore day rolls over at 2 AM.
- Admin dashboard remembers your scroll position per menu.
- Home button for kiosk layouts and a hidden Parent tab option.

## Updating
Update in HACS, restart Home Assistant, then hard-refresh your dashboards.
