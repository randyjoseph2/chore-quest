# Chore Quest 3.6.0

## What's new
- **Fines.** Fine a racer from the Cup or Parent tab with a reason, locked behind the Parent PIN.
- **Banner.** The fine shows in the Nabu Flow alert bar for 15 minutes, like prizes, and is sent as a notification.

## Recently
- Card loads as a dashboard resource (3.5.3), retry and remembered state (3.5.2), dashboard theme fills the screen (3.5.1).

## Updating
Update in HACS, restart Home Assistant, then hard-refresh your dashboards.
